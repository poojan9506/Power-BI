# 📱 Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard built on mobile phone sales data to track sales performance across brands, cities, payment methods and time.

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Quantity, Transactions, Average Sale
- **Slicers:** Month, Mobile Model, Payment Method, Brand, Day Name
- **Total Sales by City** – map visual
- **Total Quantity by Month** – trend line chart
- **Customer Ratings** – funnel chart
- **Transactions by Payment Method** – pie chart
- **Total Sales by Mobile Model** – bar chart
- **Total Sales by Day Name** – area chart
- **Brand Summary Table** – sales, quantity, transactions

## 📁 Dataset
- 3,835 transactions | 2021–2024 | 5 brands | 15 mobile models | 19 cities
- Columns: Transaction ID, Date, Brand, Mobile Model, Units Sold, Price Per Unit, Customer Name, Age, City, Payment Method, Customer Ratings

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Apple and Samsung are the top brands by revenue
- Delhi and Mumbai contribute the largest share of sales
- UPI is the top payment method by revenue

## 🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query, Microsoft Excel
